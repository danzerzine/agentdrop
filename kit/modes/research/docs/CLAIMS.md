# CLAIMS — what we assert and how sure we are

_Every claim that reaches `STATE.md`, a deck or people outside the project. The level changes only here, with the reason. Before a claim leaves the project, an independent skeptic tries to refute it._

Levels: **fact** (on our data, reproducible), **observation** (seen in data, mechanism unknown), **hypothesis** (an explanation we have not tested), **opinion** (someone else's, with the source).
