# CLAIMS — what we assert and how sure we are

_Every claim that reaches `STATE.md`, a deck or people outside the project. The level changes only here, with the reason. Before a claim leaves the project, an independent skeptic tries to refute it._

Levels: **fact** (reproduced from raw data, no "because"), **observation** (seen in our data, cause unknown), **hypothesis** (an explanation, fit for an experiment), **opinion** (someone else's, with the source).

Skeptic's verdict: **holds**, **holds narrowed**, **withdrawn**, **can't check**; **not checked** until the skeptic has looked. Rows are numbered C1, C2… and never renumbered; a withdrawn claim keeps its row.
