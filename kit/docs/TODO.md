# TODO — tickets

_One item = one ticket `B<n>` with priority P0–P3: only what remains to do and where it came from; history goes to the spec or the commit. Numbers are never reused. Right under its title every ticket has a one-line summary, `Summary: …` (`Коротко: …`): what it is about in plain words, at most 100 characters, no task codes, file paths or jargon. When done, the item leaves and `LOG.md` gets "B<n> — done, commit …". A large ticket gets a spec in `docs/specs/B<n>-<name>.md`. Questions for people go to `QUESTIONS.md`._
